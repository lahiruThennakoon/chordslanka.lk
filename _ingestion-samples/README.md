# Ingestion spike samples

Drop 8–10 chord sheet files here (JPEG or PDF) for the OQ-3 ingestion spike.

**Pick them deliberately, not conveniently.** The spike measures three numbers —
chord accuracy against the source, minutes of human review per sheet, and
conversion cost per sheet — and OQ-3 sets abort thresholds in advance so the
result cannot be rationalised afterwards. Sampling only the clean scans would
produce a comfortable answer that tells us nothing about the other 4,990 sheets.

A useful sample spans:

- 2–3 of the cleanest, most legible sheets
- 2–3 typical sheets, whatever "typical" looks like in the bank
- 2–3 of the worst still worth publishing — skewed, faint, handwritten, photographed rather than scanned
- at least one PDF and at least one JPEG
- at least one sheet in each script present in the catalogue

Filenames do not matter. Nothing here is published, and this folder is excluded
from version control.
