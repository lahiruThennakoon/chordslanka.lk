03

First Principles Thinking

Strip it back. What is irreducibly true about a chord sheet?

  1. A song must default to its original scale or key. The original is the starting truth, not a user preference.
  2. The user must see the full chord sheet at once, in readable fonts. Readability is a requirement, not a setting.
  3. The user must be able to transpose from that original key.

Technique 05 · the centre of the session

Five Whys

It started with something small and slightly annoying: people screenshot chord sheets instead of coming back to the site. Five questions later it was not about screenshots at all.

  1. The observation

    People screenshot chords instead of returning to the site.

  2. Why? — one

    Because searching again every time is not easy. The screenshot is a shortcut past repeat search.

    idea Give users a favourites list they can add to and remove from, so they never re-search a song they already found. And let them build their own collections by occasion and theme.

  3. Why? — two

    Because typing a song name, or any delay at all, kills the enthusiasm of a trip or party singalong. The moment is social and it does not wait.

  4. Why? — three

    Because during that pause someone in the group just starts singing, in whatever pitch they landed on — and the guitarist is left chasing that key by ear.

    insight The singer sets the key, not the guitarist. The product has to be able to catch up to a key that is already in the air.

  5. Why? — four

    Because only a pro can find the key by ear quickly. Everyone else cannot. Following a singer is currently a skill gate, not a tool.

  6. Why? — five · root cause

    Because the missing thing is years of experience. Following a singer by ear is earned. It cannot be taught in the moment.

The screenshot habit traces all the way down to a skill gap — and a skill gap is something a product can stand in for.

Fishbone Diagram

A user finds wrong chords on a page. Where did that come from?

Cause

Wrong chords come from the source that was extracted. The error is inherited from whoever made the original sheet — not introduced by us.

Consequence

The 5,000-sheet bank carries unknown accuracy. Conversion copies both the chords and the mistakes.

Which is why

The suggest-a-correction path from experienced players — drawn out back in technique 02 — is not a nicety. It is the only repair mechanism the product has.

Build on What Works

What is already working, that you can build on?

idea There are many Sinhala chord sites already, and the founder believes their main income is AdSense. The model is proven locally — and the founder wants to out-earn them.

the founder asked directly How do we earn more profit from AdSense on this kind of site?

↗ External research — not the founder's ideas

What was looked up, in answer to that question

Sri Lankan audience, reported $1 – $3 RPM
Same content, US / UK / CA / AU readers $5 – $25 RPM
  • Music is an entry-level AdSense niche — roughly $4 to $10 RPM worldwide, driven by about 1.2% CTR and $0.35 CPC. Country mix matters more than niche. Local-only traffic caps earnings hard.
  • Google Publisher Policies ban content that infringes copyright, and publishers are responsible for user-generated content on ad-bearing pages. Reproducing full lyrics without a reprint licence is the stated approval and suspension risk.
  • AdSense guidance for music sites warns against copied lyrics, thin pages, ads beside players, and CTR above about 2%, which can look like accidental clicks.
  • Mediavine Journey now accepts sites from about 1,000 monthly sessions; Ezoic requires about 250,000 monthly users for new publishers. A higher-paying network later is a real lever — AdSense need not be permanent.

Implication for profit: the levers are audience country mix, pages per session, original written content around the songs, and page speed — not ad density. These figures are reported ranges from external sources, brought into the session to answer a direct question. Everything else on this page came out of the founder.

Technique 25 · Scenario Cross · the synthesis

Five things the session was actually about

Everything above was the founder talking. These five are what all of it adds up to when you lay the answers next to each other.

Synthesis 01

The missing feature the founder described without ever naming it

The Five Whys root cause was experience — only a pro can catch a key a singer has already started in. Original-key default, plus transpose, plus that root cause, add up to a key-catching helper that tells the player which way to move. Nothing in the feature list does this yet. It is the one place the product can replace years of ear training.

Synthesis 02

The cut list is more truthful than the fallback

The AI analyser and tuner are what the founder reached for when the catalog vanished — but the founder also named them as removable without losing the weekly habit. Catalog, search, favourites, transpose and autoscroll are load-bearing. The tools are not.

Synthesis 03

Two users, only one of them urgent

Favourites and collections serve the returning user. But the emotionally urgent moment is the gathering, entered from a Google search. Song-page ranking and first-load speed serve that moment; favourites do not. Both worth building — only one urgent.

Synthesis 04 · the core tension

The differentiator is also the lowest-paying and highest-risk content

Local Sinhala and Tamil repertoire is what makes this site worth existing. It is also the content that earns least and risks most.

What makes it special Local Sinhala and Tamil repertoire — the reason anyone would choose this site over a global one.
against
What it costs Reportedly $1–$3 RPM against $5–$25 for Western traffic — and full lyrics are the exact content Google publisher policy names, with the publisher liable for user submissions too.
Synthesis 05

The one move that survives every future

Original written content around each song. It makes pages defensible if AdSense refuses, lifts pages per session if traffic stays local, ranks in English if traffic goes international, and creates ad inventory if approved. It was the only move that held up across all four scenario-cross futures.
