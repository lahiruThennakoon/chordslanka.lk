03

One Feature Only

If only one capability ships: the phone play view. Beat the WhatsApp notebook photo — not Ultimate Guitar’s feature matrix.

  1. The one

    Readable lyrics, chords locked to the right syllable, thumb-reachable transpose.

  2. Second · worth being great at

    Songs I can play: known chords in, playable songs out.

  3. Third · trust is a feature

    Verified stamp: which recording, who checked it, when.

  4. Engine first

    Transposition as a pure function with tests before any interface. Capo = transpose-by-fret with two labels: key that sounds / shapes you play.

  5. Validation cut · excludes

    AI, community publishing, tuner, metronome, strumming animation, playlists, teacher tools. Accounts wait until a stranger finishes a song without one.

05

Kill the Crown Jewel

Delete the sacred cow. Watch what survives — and what must never be deleted.

✗

Delete the giant library

Product becomes a coach for thirty songs. Acquisition harder; learning quality spikes. Depth is the proof. Breadth is a later moat.

✗

Delete lyrics

Legal risk drops; singer-guitarists leave. Model lyrics so they can be removed per song without breaking the chord timeline.

✗

Delete interactivity

You might still get search traffic, but not a habit. SEO page and play mode are two renderings of one arrangement.

✗

Delete accounts

First session still works. Local storage can hold key, capo, and font until accounts exist.

✗

Delete AI

Product can still be excellent. AI is off the critical path to validation.

!

Cannot delete: Sinhala & Tamil

Delete them and you become a worse Ultimate Guitar. Local repertoire is the strategy — not a translation layer added later.

!

Cannot delete: transpose

Beginners who cannot sing in the original key churn. Transpose is part of the minimum, not polish.

✗

Delete chord diagrams (mostly)

Intermediates survive. Beginners stall on the first barre or F. Keep a peek, not a textbook.

Reverse Brainstorming

How the product fails — the kill log that design must prevent.

Product failure console · do not ship these
FAIL-01Chords do not match the version in the room: film, acoustic, live, karaoke.
FAIL-02One song is one sheet — hits have more than one arrangement people mean by the title.
FAIL-03Sinhala search misses the Singlish spelling people actually type.
FAIL-04A beginner song uses barre shapes because the transcriber plays intermediate voicings.
FAIL-05Scroll speed follows a recorded BPM and the singer breathes late.
FAIL-06A login wall sits in front of the first chord.
FAIL-07Lyrics copied at scale; a publisher writes before there are users.
FAIL-08An AI sheet is public with no human ear on the recording.
FAIL-09A tuner is promised; iOS Safari makes it feel broken — brand becomes the broken tuner.
FAIL-10Empty programmatic pages (every chord-name URL) get indexed.
FAIL-11Ultimate Guitar’s tab / rating / version maze squeezed onto a narrow phone.
FAIL-12Moderation is English-only; submissions are Sinhala and Tamil.
FAIL-13Chords stored as display strings — C♯/D♭, slash chords, N.C. cannot round-trip through transpose.
FAIL-14Capo is a text note; transpose + capo shifts the song twice.
FAIL-15The paid tier is the one that makes the song readable.
FAIL-16Comments under a song become arguments about lyrics and politics instead of a wrong chord.

Share links

“Nearby”

WhatsApp

Campus / shops

08

Fusion Cuisine

Local repertoire as written memory — three scripts, one catalog promise.

සි

Sinhala · Singlish · Unicode

Search accepts Sinhala Unicode and common romanized Singlish; stores a normalized romanization index; displays the native title. Primary title stays in original script.

த

Tamil · Tanglish · Latin

Same promise. Tamil is a first-class catalog — not a tab on a Sinhala site.

En

English aliases · artists · lines

Artists need nicknames and shortened spellings. An English alias is a search key, not a replacement name. Lines can switch language mid-lyric; chord anchors attach to words or grapheme clusters.

  • Many Sinhala songs live as oral memory and online video — the product becomes the written memory.
  • Genre is local-first: film, baila, calypso, pop, choir and worship, campus, oldies, new video hits. Map to global genres later.
  • Worship guitarists are a dense recurring segment — discover service setlists as a job; do not dump a worship catalog until rights are understood.
  • Trending means played here this week from on-site play starts — not a copied global chart.
  • Teach a strum first as spoken down-up syllables, including a Sinhala mnemonic, before any animation.
  • Code-switching and romanized chorus lines need their own alias lines so search finds the phrase people sing.

11

Utopia vs Dystopia

Brochure future on the left. Landfill future on the right. The move that survives both.

Utopia · the brochure

  • Hum a chorus — get a sheet.
  • Personal practice sequence from changes you miss — without ingesting a copyrighted master.
  • Romanization and alias suggestions machine-proposed, human-confirmed (catalog tool, not magic button).
  • Difficulty, similar progressions, barre class computed from structured chords first; call a model only where rules run out.
  • Model output stores provenance: model, version, reviewer, time. Screen says when a human has not checked.
  • Report a wrong chord (or wrong suggestion) in two taps → editorial ticket, not a comment thread.

Dystopia · the landfill

  • Wrong chords outranking the good ones.
  • Leading the brand with AI chord extraction invites a marketing claim and a legal claim.
  • A microphone that judges your playing — different product (pedagogy, latency, rights). Park it.
  • A striped catalog where some songs are chord-only and some are full sheets — users grumble.
Survives both futures Never auto-publishing model output. The dystopia of a striped catalog (chord-only where lyrics uncleared; full sheet where cleared) is still better than a lawsuit — inconsistency is an adult policy.

Backcasting

Start from the dictionary future. Walk back to the first thirty songs.

Three years out

A guitarist in Sri Lanka opens this for the song the way they open a dictionary. Sheets match the recording. Play mode survives a gig. Corrections stick.

Year one

A few hundred verified songs people actually request + play mode, transpose, capo, three-script search, artist pages.

First move · now

Thirty songs chosen by asking guitarists what they get asked to play — transcribed carefully, delightful on a phone. Each song page targets the query people type: song name + chords, in their script.

Why they return

Key and capo still there. The change they failed is waiting. Saturday’s set is one tap away.

The moat

Verified local corpus, alignment quality, alias search — not a tuner.

Global later

Reuse play mode; add a corpus where diaspora demand exists. Cloning English pop is a red ocean.

Money · later

Do not paywall the sheet. Free = full play of verified catalog. Payment = offline, quiet browsing, setlists across devices. Gear affiliates and teacher directory wait until trust exists — disclosed, optional, never in play view.

Smallest validation

Thirty songs, play, transpose, capo, search — watched with twenty guitarists on their own phones. Success: finishing a song and opening a second unprompted. Willingness to pay is open until the play habit exists.

Morphological Analysis

The shape of the product: identity, lines, versions, engines, surfaces.

Dimension Choice
Home Continue, short local trending, search — not a magazine.
Primary nav Search, song, play, practice, your library. Artist / genre / language are doors for SEO and browsing — not main tabs.
Identity Song (aliases, languages) → Recording/Version (key, tempo, suggested capo, source, verifier) → Arrangement revision (sections, lines).
Line model Lyric tokens + chord events at a token index. Chord event structured; display text derived.
Versions First-class. One canonical song URL with a switcher. Alternates do not compete as separate indexable duplicates.
Diagrams Keyed by chord, tuning, voicing — not baked into song text.
Transpose Semitones to pitch classes; respell toward target key; keep slash bass; leave N.C. and percussion alone. User transpose + capo compose — system solves shapes.
Search Analyzers for Sinhala, Tamil, English + romanization field. Chord search structured: contains, count, no barre, tuning.
Revisions Immutable and cacheable. Play payload = that revision. Edit publishes new. Old share links keep revision or follow latest — chosen on purpose.
Render Server-render the song so the first verse is visible on a slow phone and to a crawler. Play hydrates the same arrangement. No canvas-drawn sheet.
Client / data One web client as installable site. Shared pure module for transpose/capo. Single relational DB. No microservices. Object storage not for audio masters.
API shape Public reads: song, search, artist. Writes at first: error reports. Admin: verify, unpublish, revert. Auth can wait.
Type System fonts covering Sinhala and Tamil (or hosted subset). A Latin-only web font that delays the lyric is a defect.
A11y / measure Reduced motion = no auto-scroll. Chord identity not color alone. Desktop key paths for search and transpose. Measure: open, play start/complete, transpose, capo, error report, zero-result queries (editorial backlog). Do not record microphone audio.

Provocation Technique

Deliberately absurd posters — then keep what is useful.

The homepage is only the song you are learning.

Retention likes it. Discovery and SEO still need other doors. Split the surfaces.

License lyrics for a top local hundred — make that the press story.

Slow, expensive, and the real moat if a publisher will talk.

Ship chord-only for uncleared international covers popular locally; full sheets for cleared or original local work.

Adult policy — even if the catalog looks striped.

User sings one comfort note once; song offers the nearest singable key.

That audio is theirs, not a master. Still post-validation.

Home-screen drill is the chord change you fail most often.

Practice comes to you.

Section carries a text strum pattern such as “down down-up.”

Useful and easy enough to keep. Animation is a toy and waits.

Standard tuning only — the engine must sound for launch.

Other tunings: label + warning, or song stays unpublished. No custom tuning studio.

Personal notes, cross-device history, reputation need accounts.

Do not fake them with device-only data and call it a profile.

Worst Possible Idea

Invent the disaster launch — then invert it into the minimum and the roadmap.

A scraper, an AI publisher, a tuner, and a paywall on the chorus.
A human-checked phone sheet, free to play, with tools postponed.
Differentiation: “we have AI.”
A phone playbook for Sri Lankan songs, checked by guitarists, in the script you search and the key you sing.
Accuracy process: whoever typed last.
Two guitarists against a named recording before the sheet is verified.
Moderation: open wiki on day one.
Small editor group, version history, and revert. Reputation after editors exist.
SEO: thousands of chord / key / capo combination URLs.
Real songs, real artists, hubs a human would browse.
Monetization: premium chords.
Correctness is not a luxury feature.

Keep for the minimum

Verified songs; search by title, artist, lyric fragment, and language; song page; play mode with large type, wake lock, section jump, and manual advance; transpose; capo with sounding versus played; diagram peek; dark play; font size; report-a-wrong-chord; artist pages.

Next after that minimum

Accounts, favorites, setlists, remembered key and capo, a practice path on one song, chord-vocabulary filter, better romanization, trending from real plays, cache of recent songs.

Later still

Community diffs, editor tools at scale, premium offline, push for Tamil corpus parity, classroom or worship only if rights are clear, deterministic similar songs, then tuner and metronome.

Experimental only

Practice plan from failed changes, labeled alternate voicings, audio extraction after counsel.

Postpone · impressive, low-value early

Strumming animation, custom tunings, audio extraction, community reputation, teacher dashboards, social feeds, and comments.

Synthesis · the last chorus

What this session decided

The single most important problem

I cannot get a trustworthy, readable, singable chord sheet for the song in front of me, in the script I search with, and keep my place while I play.

Chosen differentiation: verified Sri Lankan repertoire, phone play mode that respects a guitar in your hands, alias and script search, and honest versions. AI is not the wedge.

Chosen MVP · in

  • About thirty guitarist-requested songs
  • Logged-out search and song page
  • Play mode
  • Deterministic transpose
  • Deterministic capo
  • Diagram peek
  • Error report
  • Artist pages

Explicitly out

  • Accounts
  • AI
  • User-generated sheets
  • Audio upload
  • YouTube ingest
  • Tuner
  • Metronome
  • Strumming animation
  • Ads inside play
  • Paywalled chords
Deterministic forever Chord structure, transpose, capo composition, spelling, diagrams, barre and easy-voicing class, progression similarity. Models may only suggest, must be labeled, and must not auto-publish.
Legal direction Counsel before lyrics go wide; lyrics removable per song; takedown path live at launch; no master uploads; no YouTube ripping; contributor terms before any public editing; feasibility is not permission.
SEO direction Index songs and artists and a few human hubs; noindex facets and drafts; long-tail lives on the song page; zero-result searches become the transcription queue. Largest useful catalog, not largest page count.
Money direction Free includes full play of verified songs. Ads, if any, only on browse and never shifting play mode. Later payment is offline packs, ad-free browse, and synced setlists. Teachers and affiliates wait.
Retention Comes from remembered key and capo, setlists, the failed chord change, and a next song that uses shapes you have. Catalog tourism does not.
Cold start An editorial sprint. First build: play view and transpose engine, then the thirty songs. Architecture: server-rendered site + relational store + pure chord module.
Global later Same product and a new corpus driven by diaspora demand — not a clone of English tab sites.
