- (decision) Stance is autonomous (ideate for me): the brief asks a discovery team to produce the product definition, not to facilitate the user's ideas.
- (decision) No unfinished memlogs. No project-context.md. Repository has BMAD scaffolding and no product README, so this is greenfield discovery.
- (direction) Working name SongChords. Initial market Sri Lanka. Languages Sinhala, Tamil, English. Do not write production code in this session.
- (technique) started Assumption Reversal
- (idea by coach) A small set of perfectly playable Sri Lankan songs beats an empty promise of a large library. Launch promise: songs you can finish, not songs we indexed.
- (idea by coach) The product is the twenty minutes between opening the phone and getting through the chorus without stopping. Time-to-first-clean-chorus is the north-star, not catalog size.
- (idea by coach) Most beginners do not know the word capo. The control they want is make this easier to sing. Capo is an advanced disclosure of that job.
- (idea by coach) UI language and lyric language are different settings. Chrome follows the user. The song stays in its own script.
- (idea by coach) Open community publishing makes chord sites untrustworthy. Launch curated and verified. The community proposes a correction, not a new canonical sheet.
- (idea by coach) AI chord extraction is the fastest way to publish wrong chords at scale. AI may draft behind a reviewer. It is never the public source of truth.
- (idea by coach) Ultimate Guitar already won English pop tabs. The hole is the Sinhala and Tamil song a guitarist in Colombo has to play tonight.
- (idea by coach) A guitarist on a bus searches a fragment of a chorus they half remember, often in Singlish, not by musical key.
- (idea by coach) Play mode must work logged out on the first visit or search traffic bounces. An account is memory: key, capo, progress. It is not the gate.
- (idea by coach) Teachers and music schools are a later segment. There is no teacher supply until students have a habit. The first payer is a hobbyist who wants offline and no ads while playing.
- (idea by coach) Chord diagrams during play steal the lyric line. Diagrams are a peek, not a docked panel, once the song has started.
- (idea by coach) Difficulty is not a property of the song alone. It is the pair of this player and this song: barre, speed, and whether they have to sing.

- (technique) started One Feature Only
- (idea by coach) If only one capability ships, it is a phone play view: readable lyrics, chords locked to the right syllable, thumb-reachable transpose.
- (idea by coach) The thing to beat is a photo of a notebook in a WhatsApp group, not a feature matrix against Ultimate Guitar.
- (idea by coach) The second capability worth being great at is songs I can play: known chords in, playable songs out.
- (idea by coach) The third is a verified stamp: which recording, who checked it, and when. Trust is a feature.
- (idea by coach) Search plus a static chord page will not beat existing lyric sites. The wedge is playability while holding a guitar.
- (idea by coach) Build transposition as a pure function with tests before any interface. One wrong transpose destroys trust permanently.
- (idea by coach) Capo is not a separate product. It is transpose-by-fret with two labels: the key that sounds and the shapes you play.
- (idea by coach) A one-feature validation cut excludes AI, community publishing, tuner, metronome, strumming animation, playlists, and teacher tools.
- (idea by coach) Accounts, favorites, and practice history are memory features. They wait until a stranger can finish a song without an account.
- (technique) started Crank the Dial to 11
- (idea by coach) Play mode is a stage, not a document page. One column. Current line high enough to see after a glance down. Next line only as preview. Everything else leaves.
- (idea by coach) Auto-scroll is what people request and what loses them when singing drifts. A huge advance-line zone is more honest. Offer auto-scroll as optional, set by feel, not by typing a BPM.
- (idea by coach) Section chips for verse, chorus, and bridge matter more than smooth pixel scroll, because practice is sectional.
- (idea by coach) Screen wake lock is mandatory. If the browser refuses, say so in one line. A dark phone at the second verse is a failed product.
- (idea by coach) Font size persists on the device. Chord alignment must survive the size change. Alignment, not the slider, is the hard layout problem.
- (idea by coach) Design landscape first. A phone on a music stand is sideways. Portrait is the hand-held fallback.
- (idea by coach) Playing is one-handed interaction. Put every control in the bottom band or the edges. The fretting hand does not pinch-zoom.
- (idea by coach) Announce a chord only when it changes. A beat-by-beat highlight fights the singing.
- (idea by coach) Chord-only view is for players who know the words. Lyrics-only is a weak product and a sharper legal question. Do not build a separate lyric catalog.
- (idea by coach) Dark, high-contrast play is the default for night. Gray on gray fails under stage light and bedside lamps alike.
- (idea by coach) If a metronome exists later, it clicks in the ear. A visual metronome fights the line you are reading.
- (idea by coach) No ads, upgrades, or rating prompts inside play mode. Interrupting the job is a broken product, even if the ad network prefers it.
- (idea by coach) The arrangement you opened should stay on the device. A dead spot in a hall cannot blank the chorus.
- (idea by coach) Stand mode assumes the phone is a meter away: enormous type and section advance. A Bluetooth pedal is a later joke that might be serious.
- (idea by coach) Performance mode hides practice chrome and shows only songs marked ready. Weddings and services are a different anxiety than practice.
- (technique) started Kill the Crown Jewel
- (idea by coach) Delete the giant library and the product becomes a coach for thirty songs. Acquisition gets harder and learning quality spikes. Depth is the proof. Breadth is a later moat.
- (idea by coach) Delete lyrics and legal risk drops while singer-guitarists leave. Model lyrics so they can be removed per song without breaking the chord timeline.
- (idea by coach) Delete interactivity and you might still get search traffic, but not a habit. The SEO page and play mode are two renderings of one arrangement.
- (idea by coach) Delete accounts and the first session still works. Local storage can hold key, capo, and font until accounts exist.
- (idea by coach) Delete AI and the product can still be excellent. AI is off the critical path to validation.
- (idea by coach) Delete Sinhala and Tamil and you become a worse Ultimate Guitar. Local repertoire is the strategy, not a translation layer added later.
- (idea by coach) Delete transpose and beginners who cannot sing in the original key churn. Transpose is part of the minimum, not polish.
- (idea by coach) Delete chord diagrams and intermediates survive. Beginners stall on the first barre or the first F. Keep a peek, not a textbook.

- (technique) started Cross-Pollination
- (idea by coach) Steal karaoke's confidence about which line you are on. Do not steal a bouncing ball on every word. Bounce the chord change.
- (idea by coach) Steal a lesson path from language apps: meet the chord, meet the change, meet the section, then the song. A streak is songs touched this week, not guilt.
- (idea by coach) Steal the local habit of a cricket score app: fast on a bad connection, huge type, one job on screen.
- (idea by coach) Steal the recipe-site correction: the sheet is above the fold. The story about the song is optional and below.
- (idea by coach) Steal offline maps: download this song, download this set, see the storage it uses.
- (idea by coach) Steal spaced repetition only for the chord change the player actually misses, not a separate course product.
- (idea by coach) A share link opens play mode already in the agreed key and capo so a band is on the same shapes. No account required.
- (idea by coach) A teacher later sends one song link and each student keeps a key. Do not build the class product first.
- (idea by coach) Songs in my chord vocabulary is the nearby feature. Geography is irrelevant. Known shapes are the location.
- (idea by coach) Every sheet cites a source the way a reference does: recording, transcriber, verifier, date.
- (idea by coach) Meet the notebook photo where it already lives. A later distribution move is a WhatsApp share of the play link, not a chatbot that ingests audio.
- (idea by coach) Tuition shops and campus guitar circles are the zero-budget channel: a request list and a sticker, not ads.
- (technique) started Fusion Cuisine
- (idea by coach) Many Sinhala songs live as oral memory and online video, not as an authoritative book. The product becomes the written memory.
- (idea by coach) Search accepts Sinhala Unicode and common romanized Singlish, stores a normalized romanization index, and displays the native title.
- (idea by coach) Tamil is the same promise: Tamil script, Tanglish, and Latin. Tamil is a first-class catalog, not a tab on a Sinhala site.
- (idea by coach) Artists need aliases. People search nicknames, shortened names, and spellings that do not match the formal credit.
- (idea by coach) Genre is local-first: film, baila, calypso, pop, choir and worship, campus, oldies, new video hits. Map to global genres later. Do not start from a Western taxonomy.
- (idea by coach) Worship guitarists are a dense recurring segment. Treat service setlists as a discovered job. Do not dump a worship catalog until rights are understood.
- (idea by coach) A line can switch language mid-lyric. Chord anchors attach to words or grapheme clusters, not to Latin characters.
- (idea by coach) The primary title stays in the original script. An English alias is a search key, not a replacement name.
- (idea by coach) Trending means played here this week, from on-site play starts, not a copied global chart.
- (idea by coach) Teach a strum first as spoken down-up syllables, including a Sinhala mnemonic, before any animation exists.
- (idea by coach) Code-switching and romanized chorus lines need their own alias lines so search finds the phrase people sing, not only the official title.
- (technique) started Constraint Mapping
- (idea by coach) Lyrics are the hard copyright. Chord names are safer and still not a free pass when locked to the full lyric. Counsel decides. The model keeps lyrics removable.
- (idea by coach) Technical ability to extract chords from audio is not legal permission. Feasibility and permission stay separate in every AI idea.
- (idea by coach) Do not accept full-song uploads in the minimum product. Storage of masters, even temporary, is a rights and a security problem.
- (idea by coach) Fetching audio from a YouTube URL can break the platform terms even when the user supplies the link. Link out to the video. Do not ingest it.
- (idea by coach) Publish a takedown contact, a form, a log, and a fast unpublish before the catalog is public. Map the process to Sri Lankan law with counsel, and still look familiar to foreign publishers.
- (idea by coach) No public submission without a contributor agreement: license to display, a representation, a warranty, and the right to moderate or refuse.
- (idea by coach) Practice history stays out of any sale or ad profile. Analytics can be logged out and query-level.
- (idea by coach) Sinhala search must normalize equivalent Unicode or the same title misses. Tamil normalization is its own work, not a copy of the Sinhala rules.
- (idea by coach) A play payload is compact structured data. Diagrams are shared drawings, not a photograph per chord.
- (idea by coach) Microphone features on mobile browsers are a swamp. Tuner and listen-to-me practice wait until play mode is loved.
- (idea by coach) The imagined constraint of matching every Ultimate Guitar feature should be dissolved. The real constraint is chord accuracy, and it is the brand: checked by a guitarist.
- (idea by coach) Two scripts and romanized search are a constraint that becomes the acquisition advantage on queries global sites ignore.
- (idea by coach) A paper escape hatch: one action exports the current key for a place that bans phones. Same legal content, different surface.
- (technique) started TRIZ Contradiction
- (idea by coach) Coverage fights accuracy. Resolve it with states: draft, reviewed, verified. Search and the index show verified by default. Drafts are never indexable.
- (idea by coach) Landing pages fight thin content. Index the song, the artist, and a few real hubs such as language. Faceted filter URLs are noindex.
- (idea by coach) Free growth fights a clean play screen. Ads may sit on browse and search. Play mode stays clean. Premium later removes browse ads and adds offline packs and synced setlists.
- (idea by coach) Transpose must be instant and correct on ugly chords. Store root, quality, extensions, and bass. An unparsed annotation is left untouched and flagged, never guessed.
- (idea by coach) Beginners need shapes and players need words. Show a diagram the first time a chord appears in that song, then the symbol only.
- (idea by coach) Community speed fights moderation cost. A correction is a structured change at a chord anchor, not a replacement essay.
- (idea by coach) Sri Lanka now fights a global later. The data model is locale-neutral. The launch content and navigation are Sri Lankan. There is no second codebase.
- (idea by coach) Helpful AI fights trust. Transpose, capo, diagrams, key math, and voicing class stay deterministic. A model may suggest a practice order or an easier shape, labeled as a suggestion, and may not silently rewrite a verified sheet.
- (idea by coach) SEO wants many URLs and quality wants few. Long-tail capo and easy versions are sections on the song page, not extra URLs.
- (idea by coach) A no-barre filter fights the fact that barre is a voicing choice. A song is no-barre only if every chord has an open shape in the stated tuning.
- (technique) started Utopia vs Dystopia Split-Screen
- (idea by coach) The brochure future hears a hummed chorus and returns a sheet. The headline future is a landfill of wrong chords outranking the good ones. The move that survives both is never auto-publishing model output.
- (idea by coach) A personal practice sequence based on changes you miss is a real assistance feature and does not require ingesting a copyrighted master.
- (idea by coach) Leading the brand with AI chord extraction invites both a marketing claim and a legal claim. Do not lead with it.
- (idea by coach) Romanization and alias suggestions can be machine-proposed and human-confirmed. That is a catalog tool, not a user-facing magic button.
- (idea by coach) Difficulty, similar progressions, and barre classification should be computed from the structured chords first. Call a model only where the rules run out.
- (idea by coach) A microphone that judges your playing is a different product. It needs pedagogy, latency, and a rights story. Park it.
- (idea by coach) Any model output stores provenance: model, version, reviewer, and time. The screen says when a human has not checked it.
- (idea by coach) Users can report a wrong chord, including a wrong suggestion, in two taps. That report is an editorial ticket, not a comment thread.
- (idea by coach) The dystopia of a striped catalog is still better than a lawsuit: chord-only where lyrics are not cleared, full sheet where they are. Inconsistency is an adult policy, even if users grumble.

- (technique) started Morphological Analysis
- (idea by coach) Home is continue, a short local trending list, and search. It is not a magazine.
- (idea by coach) Primary navigation is search, the song, play, practice, and your library. Artist, genre, and language pages are doors for search engines and for browsing, not the main tabs.
- (idea by coach) Identity is Song, with aliases and languages. A Recording or Version has key, tempo, suggested capo, source, and verifier. An Arrangement revision has sections and lines.
- (idea by coach) A line is lyric tokens plus chord events anchored at a token index. A chord event is structured. Display text is derived.
- (idea by coach) Multiple versions are first-class and share one canonical song URL with a switcher. Alternate versions do not compete as separate indexable duplicates.
- (idea by coach) Diagrams are keyed by chord, tuning, and voicing. They are not baked into the song text.
- (idea by coach) Transpose adds semitones to pitch classes, respells toward the target key, keeps slash bass, and leaves N.C. and percussion marks alone.
- (idea by coach) User transpose and capo compose. The player states the key that should sound and the fret of the capo. The system solves the shapes. It does not apply both as blind shifts the user must undo.
- (idea by coach) Text search uses analyzers for Sinhala, Tamil, and English plus a romanization field. Chord search is structured: contains, count, no barre, tuning.
- (idea by coach) An arrangement revision is immutable and cacheable. A play payload is that revision. An edit publishes a new revision. Old share links keep their revision or explicitly follow latest, chosen on purpose.
- (idea by coach) Server-render the song so the first verse is visible on a slow phone and visible to a crawler. Play mode hydrates the same arrangement. Do not draw the sheet on a canvas.
- (idea by coach) One web client as an installable site. Shared pure module for transpose and capo so a native client later does not invent a second engine.
- (idea by coach) A single relational database. No microservices. Object storage is not for audio masters.
- (idea by coach) Public reads are song, search, and artist. Writes at first are error reports. Admin writes are verify, unpublish, and revert. Auth can wait.
- (idea by coach) System fonts that already cover Sinhala and Tamil, or a subset font you host. A latin-only web font that delays the lyric is a defect.
- (idea by coach) Reduced motion means no auto-scroll. Chord identity is not color alone. Desktop key paths exist for search and transpose even though the design is mobile-first.
- (idea by coach) Measure song open, play start, play complete, transpose, capo set, error report, and zero-result queries. Zero-result queries are the editorial backlog. Do not record microphone audio.

- (insight) The single most important problem is: I cannot get a trustworthy, readable, singable chord sheet for the song in front of me, in the script I search with, and keep my place while I play.
- (insight) Chosen differentiation: verified Sri Lankan repertoire, phone play mode that respects a guitar in your hands, alias and script search, and honest versions. AI is not the wedge.
- (insight) Chosen MVP: about thirty guitarist-requested songs, logged-out search and song page, play mode, deterministic transpose, deterministic capo, diagram peek, error report, artist pages. Explicitly out: accounts, AI, user-generated sheets, audio upload, YouTube ingest, tuner, metronome, strumming animation, ads inside play, paywalled chords.
- (insight) Deterministic forever: chord structure, transpose, capo composition, spelling, diagrams, barre and easy-voicing class, progression similarity. Models may only suggest, must be labeled, and must not auto-publish.
- (insight) Legal direction: counsel before lyrics go wide; lyrics removable per song; takedown path live at launch; no master uploads; no YouTube ripping; contributor terms before any public editing; feasibility is not permission.
- (insight) SEO direction: index songs and artists and a few human hubs; noindex facets and drafts; long-tail lives on the song page; zero-result searches become the transcription queue. Largest useful catalog, not largest page count.
- (insight) Money direction: free includes full play of verified songs. Ads, if any, only on browse and never shifting play mode. Later payment is offline packs, ad-free browse, and synced setlists. Teachers and affiliates wait.
- (insight) Retention comes from remembered key and capo, setlists, the failed chord change, and a next song that uses shapes you have. Catalog tourism does not.
- (insight) Cold start is an editorial sprint. The first build is the play view and the transpose engine, then the thirty songs. Architecture stays a server-rendered site plus a relational store and a pure chord module.
- (insight) Global later is the same product and a new corpus driven by diaspora demand, not a clone of English tab sites.
