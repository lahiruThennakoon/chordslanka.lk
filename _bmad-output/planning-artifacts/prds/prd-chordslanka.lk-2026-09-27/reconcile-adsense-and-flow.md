## Gap 1.1 — No prohibition on interacting with advertisements during testing

**Severity: CRITICAL**

**What the input required (verbatim, from `development_mode.requirements`):**

> "Do not click or interact with advertisements during testing."

**Current state:** Absent from `prd.md` and `addendum.md` in every form. The closest text is SM-C1, which is about *production* click-through rate:

> "**SM-C1: Advertising click-through rate.** Must not be optimised upward. A rate above roughly 2% is treated as an invalid-activity signal by the provider and risks the account that funds the product."

SM-C1 is a counter-metric on live traffic. It says nothing about developers, QA, or automated end-to-end tests touching live ad units on a configured publisher ID. Automated browser tests that click through a page region containing a live `adsbygoogle` unit are precisely the invalid-activity pattern that gets accounts terminated — and the PRD itself classifies a provider action as existential ("a policy action is an existential event, not an inconvenience", §13.1; "Advertising account refused or suspended… Total loss of revenue; the business model disappears", §14). The single highest-consequence risk in the document has one of its two main causes unaddressed.

**Where the PRD should carry it:** §13 as a new guardrail subsection (a natural home is a new §13.5 *Advertising operations and invalid activity*), and as a consequence under FR-29, and as a row in the §14 risk table.

**Suggested fix:** Add a guardrail: *"No person and no automated test may click or interact with a live advertising unit. Test and CI environments run with advertising disabled or with placeholder slots only; a live publisher identifier is never present in any non-production environment. Self-clicks, including accidental ones during manual QA, are treated as an incident against the account, not a mistake."* Add a matching consequence to FR-29 (*"Automated tests never exercise a live advertising unit"*) and a §14 risk row: *Invalid click activity generated by testing → provider suspension → advertising disabled by default outside production; CI asserts no live publisher ID is configured.*

## Gap 1.6 — The build, lint, test, hydration, and overflow validation gate is absent

**Severity: MEDIUM**

**What the input required (verbatim, `performance.validation`):**

> "Run production build."
> "Run linting."
> "Run existing tests."
> "Run Lighthouse or equivalent performance checks where available."
> "Verify no hydration errors."
> "Verify no layout overflow."

**Current state:** Partial and weaker.

The flow diagram places `PRACTICE` between `LEARN` and `PLAY`, confirming it as a distinct stage in the founder's model rather than loose terminology.

**Current state:** FR-27 names only Play View. Practice Mode does not exist in the PRD's glossary, and §6.2 defers it along with the rest of the ladder — so there is nothing for the ban to attach to, which is why it was reasonably dropped. The residual risk is that if Practice arrives in v2, the founder's explicit ad prohibition on it lives only in a chat transcript. The `Discover Surface` definition in §3 does structurally protect it (advertising appears *only* on Discover Surfaces, an allowlist rather than a denylist), which is why this is low rather than higher.

**Where the PRD should carry it:** The §6.2 deferral bullet for the learning ladder.

**Suggested fix:** Append to that bullet: *"When Learn and Practice arrive, they are not Discover Surfaces and carry no advertising — the founder's advertising brief bans it in Practice Mode explicitly, on the same footing as Play."*
